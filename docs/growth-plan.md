# Shyam Jagat: plan to connect with people and grow

This is the plan I suggested for bringing devotees into the family and keeping them there. It was first written in the chat; this is the saved version. Nothing in it is built yet unless it says so.

## The idea in one line

Shyam devotees already come back to the temple every day and every Ekadashi. Shyam Jagat should be the place that remembers them, lets them give in a small and trusted way, and shows them what their seva did.

## The path a person takes

1. **Discover**: they arrive for something they already want (aarti times, live darshan, the Barbarik story, Ekadashi dates).
2. **Join**: a free account, with a digital card they can share.
3. **Act**: a small first seva (₹101 or ₹501) or a volunteer sign-up.
4. **Belong**: their seva in their profile, the gallery, stories, their city's team.
5. **Invite**: they share their card and seva with family on WhatsApp.

## What to build, in order

| # | What | Why | Status |
|---|------|-----|--------|
| 1 | **Ekadashi and aarti reminders** (WhatsApp or app notification, opt-in) | Brings a devotee back about twice a month, at no cost | Not built |
| 2 | **Monthly Ekadashi seva** (a recurring sankalp through Razorpay) | Steady funds, and a habit | Not built (one-time giving only today) |
| 3 | **Trust page**: 12A/80G numbers (only if the trust has them), where offerings go, receipts, annual summary | Indian donors look for this before giving | Not built. The site no longer claims 80G, 12A or FCRA until they are confirmed |
| 4 | **Proof**: photos and videos per seva, shared with the people whose wish it was | Shows the seva happened | Gallery and admin approval built; member upload not open yet |
| 5 | **Local teams**: Jaipur and Sikar/Khatu first; volunteer roles; a WhatsApp group per city | Seva is local | Volunteer sign-up built; teams not built |
| 6 | **Invite a friend and "Seva Mitra" badge** | Devotees share on WhatsApp | The digital card link exists; invites not built |
| 7 | **Falgun Mela seva campaign** (langar and volunteers) | The biggest moment of the year | Plan for early 2027; confirm dates with the temple |

## First 90 days

- **Weeks 1 to 4:** trust page, Ekadashi reminders, monthly seva. Replace sample content with real content.
- **Weeks 5 to 8:** a Jaipur and Sikar pilot of volunteers; the first real photos in the gallery (open member upload); first real stories.
- **Weeks 9 to 12:** prepare the Mela seva campaign; invite-a-friend; first review of the numbers.

## What to measure

- Sign-ups per week, and how many come back within 30 days.
- Share of new members who give a first seva within 14 days.
- Share of donors who give a second time.
- Volunteers who are approved and then actually take part.
- People who opt in to Ekadashi reminders.

## Rules the site now follows

- No invented numbers, testimonials, donors or events. Where there is nothing yet, the page says so.
- Facts about Baba Shyam and the temple come from one reference file (`src/lib/data/khatuReference.ts`), with sources listed on the About page.
- Photos and videos appear in a gallery only after an admin approves them.
